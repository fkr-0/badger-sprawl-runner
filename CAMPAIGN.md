# Campaign: Worlds, Stages, Sub-Bosses, Endbosses

## World 1 — Lower Sprawl: The Song of the Toll

Theme: rain, debt gates, undercity alleys, food stalls, cable nests.

| Stage | Name | Gameplay focus | Sub-bosses | Story beat |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1-1 | Drainpipe Wake | jump basics, claws | Toll Rat Foreman | Moss steals a wafer-key and learns the toll is charged even to children. |
| 1-2 | Neon Awning Mile | moving platforms, first shop | Scooter Bailiff, Signboard Sniper | Murr Murrby sells stims from a folding roof stall. |
| 1-3 | Cable Nest Court | first code gate | Bailiff Twins, Debt Printer Imp | Old friend Lio begs Moss to stop before people get hurt. |
| 1-4 | Market Blackout | railgun pickup, escape | Captain Grin, Drone Kennel-Master, Fuse Monk | Sister Version reveals the dub colony signal. |

Endboss: **Captain Grin, Tollbooth Saint**. A smiling armored collector riding a coin-fed barricade engine. Break armor plates with railgun, parry baton flashes, hack the toll meter mid-fight.

## World 2 — Chrome Arcology: The Badger Sells His Feet

Theme: glass elevators, indoor forests, luxury security, labor hidden behind walls.

| Stage | Name | Gameplay focus | Sub-bosses | Story beat |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2-1 | Lobby of Polished Teeth | vertical elevators | Reception Lancer | Rook Null joins as hack companion. |
| 2-2 | Atrium Orchard | enemy waves, rail angles | Holo-Gardener, Drone Wasp Queen | The trees are watered by stolen district water. |
| 2-3 | Executive Laundry | stealth belts, vents | Spin-Cycle Guard, Contract Lawyer Bot | Lio appears in uniform as corporate courier. |
| 2-4 | Elevator Seed Vault | timed heist | Glass Janitor, Vault Triplets, Panic Siren | Moss steals the elevator seed and sees workers locked below. |

Endboss: **Madame Vitrine, Mirror of Human Resources**. She turns attacks into performance reviews, summons glass copies, and offers Moss a contract mid-fight.

## World 3 — Straylight Mirage: Treason at the Mirror Banquet

Theme: orbital luxury resort, zero-g fountains, reflective traps, banquet politics.

| Stage | Name | Gameplay focus | Sub-bosses | Story beat |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3-1 | Dock of Borrowed Stars | low gravity intro | Customs Lark, Vacuum Porter | Sister Version upgrades rocket backpack for orbit. |
| 3-2 | Mirror Gallery | fake platforms | Reflection Hound, Prism Duelist | Companions argue about strategy in projected placards. |
| 3-3 | Banquet of Air | dialogue choices + combat | Sommelier Drone, Etiquette Blade, Debt Harpist | Auntie Subharmonic recognizes an old ally at the high table. |
| 3-4 | Suite of False Windows | betrayal chase | Lio the Courier, Mirror Guard Pair | Lio betrays Moss to save their family contract. |

Endboss: **The Reflection Judge**. A multi-phase mirror arbiter who copies Moss's current build, then forces a choice: expose Lio publicly or protect them and raise heat.

## World 4 — Dub Colony: The Colony Teaches the Price of Air

Theme: moving sound-system habitat, reggae/dub workshops, bass platforms, solar sails.

| Stage | Name | Gameplay focus | Sub-bosses | Story beat |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 4-1 | Speakerstack Gardens | beat platforms | Bass Beetle, Echo Drummer | The colony becomes home base. |
| 4-2 | Studio Temple | rhythm parries | Tape Priestess, Feedback Cobra | Moss learns downbeat guard. |
| 4-3 | Greenhouse Train | companion defense | Mold Angel, Rail-Rider Crew | New ally Naya Root joins as shield fighter. |
| 4-4 | Soundclash Dock | shop + wave defense | Rival Selector, Amp Golem, Static Choir | Colony votes whether to become openly rebel. |

Endboss: **King Feedback, the Friendly Tyrant**. A beloved sound leader who wants to centralize the colony for safety. Fight alternates with audience-choice dialogue.

## World 5 — Uplink Barrens: The Old Ally Wears a New Uniform

Theme: antennas, storm bridges, smuggler pylons, lightning, signal deserts.

| Stage | Name | Gameplay focus | Sub-bosses | Story beat |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 5-1 | Pylon Steppe | wind physics | Spark Jackal, Wire Witch | Rook Null detects ledger shards. |
| 5-2 | Storm Bridge | rocket precision | Thunder Bailiff, Dish-Climber Pack | Lio returns, wounded, asking for help. |
| 5-3 | Black-Ice Relay | coding minigames | Regex Fox, Packet Butcher, Null Monk | Player repairs code under pressure while Naya guards. |
| 5-4 | Smuggler Parliament | answer selection | Old Captain Ossa, Bribe Engine | The player chooses whether to trust old smugglers. |

Endboss: **The Black-Ice Fox**. A hacker-duelist who attacks with malformed commands, regex traps, and clone terminals. Perfect hacks turn its attacks into healing code.

## World 6 — Orbital Lift: The Machine That Carries Obedience

Theme: cargo containers, sky elevator, chase stages, rotating gravity.

| Stage | Name | Gameplay focus | Sub-bosses | Story beat |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 6-1 | Cargo Rosary | conveyor combat | Container Saint, Strap-Hook Twins | The rebellion sneaks into cargo prayer beads. |
| 6-2 | Customs Spine | stealth/combat hybrid | Gravity Customs, Stamp Golem | Murr Murrby runs an illegal kiosk in a customs scanner. |
| 6-3 | Cable Outside | high-altitude platforming | Wind Lancer, Maintenance Choir | Companion must be saved from falling. |
| 6-4 | Counterweight Heart | multi-companion setpiece | Balance Maw, Debt Paladin, Lift Oracle | Moss reverses cargo flow to free prisoners. |

Endboss: **The Elevator Angel**. A huge maintenance intelligence with halo-like cable arms. It is not evil; it is obedient. Dialogue can reduce its final phase.

## World 7 — Asteroid Redoubt: The Asteroid Learns to Speak

Theme: low gravity caves, rebel workshops, vacuum doors, transmitter dishes.

| Stage | Name | Gameplay focus | Sub-bosses | Story beat |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 7-1 | Rock With a Mouth | low-gravity combat | Drill Hermit | Rebels seize the first transmitter cave. |
| 7-2 | Vacuum Chapel | pressure gates | Airlock Nun, Hull Spider | Old and new allies disagree on mercy. |
| 7-3 | Rebel Foundry | crafting + defense | Forge Ox, Ammunition Ghost | Skill tree final branches unlock. |
| 7-4 | Dish Crown | full-kit platforming | Signal Lancer, Choir Splitter, Traitor Mask | The traitor is revealed as a faction, not just a person. |

Endboss: **Director Vane's Proxy Body**. A remote-operated corporate war frame. Break the frame, then fight the contract clauses projected behind it through hack gates.

## World 8 — Final Broadcast: The Audience Is Asked Who Owns the Sky

Theme: uprising montage, multi-companion assault, broadcast tower, social choice.

| Stage | Name | Gameplay focus | Sub-bosses | Story beat |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 8-1 | City Answers Back | ally routes | Riot Drone Choir, Bailiff Remnant | All rescued districts send fighters. |
| 8-2 | Ledger Under Glass | code combat | Clause Serpent, Archive Twins | Moss edits the debt ledger live. |
| 8-3 | The Treason Lesson | boss rush argument | Lio Rematch, King Feedback Echo, Vitrine Echo | Former enemies return as arguments. |
| 8-4 | Broadcast Tower | final ascent | Star Lancer, Angel Fragment, Fox Fragment | The player chooses what to broadcast. |

Final Endboss: **Director Vane, Keeper of the Sky-Lock**. Three-phase fight: bodyguard duel, contract hack, public argument. The last blow is a choice, not only an attack.
