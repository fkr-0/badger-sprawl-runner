# Story Bible

- Badger Sprawl Runner

## Premise

Moss, a tunnel-born badger courier with illegal wetware whiskers, runs contraband data through layered city sprawl. A dub-infused free colony hijacks pirate radio channels and recruits Moss for a chain of heists. Each heist exposes a corporation's orbital control network. By the end, Moss helps convert a captured satellite asteroid into a rebel broadcast fortress.

## Story arc

### Act 1 — Street teeth

Moss is hired to steal a wafer-key from the Lower Sprawl. What seems like a courier job reveals a map of orbital choke points: elevator tolls, water leases, air filters, and debt terminals. The first boss is a security captain who mocks grounders until Moss railguns the bridge controls.

### Act 2 — Heists for signal

Rook Null and Sister Version send Moss through arcology vaults, antenna storms, and a mirrored orbital resort. Each level ends in a heist object:

```yaml
heist_payloads:
  - wafer_key: "opens district routes"
  - elevator_seed: "spoofs orbital lift cargo"
  - mirror_pass: "permits entry into luxury maze"
  - bass_reactor_core: "powers rebel transmitters"
  - debt_ledger: "turns citizens from targets into allies"
```

### Act 3 — Dub colony convoy

The dub colony is not just a place, but the story driver: a moving sound-system habitat made of welded ships, greenhouse cars, studio temples, and solar sails. It carries Moss from world to world. Its bassline platforms become tutorial spaces for timing mechanics and community shops.

### Act 4 — Asteroid redoubt

The rebels seize a satellite asteroid in Earth orbit. Moss runs through low-grav tunnels, disables corp uplinks, and broadcasts the debt ledger. The final heist is not a treasure theft: it is stealing everybody's names back from the ledger.
